Notable Case Results

Matthew has secured Not Guilty verdicts on virtually every major criminal charge. Below are two recent examples that illustrate what he is capable of in a courtroom.

Not Guilty — All Charges
Attempted Murder & Aggravated Assault

Three eyewitnesses testified they personally saw the client commit the crime. Matthew exposed critical inconsistencies in their accounts and demonstrated to the jury that each witness had motive to falsely identify his client. The jury returned Not Guilty on every count.

Not Guilty — All Charges
Narcotics Distribution & Firearms Offenses

The client's own DNA was found on both an illegal firearm and a large quantity of narcotics. Matthew proved that police contaminated the evidence by failing to wear gloves during the search — and the jury returned Not Guilty on all charges.
